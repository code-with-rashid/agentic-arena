## How every lab works

1. **Predict** what the harness will do before changing a control.
2. **Configure** one policy without changing the workload.
3. **Inspect** the timeline, effect count, and stopping reason.
4. **Run** the executable fixture locally.
5. **Transfer** the contract into your own harness or framework evaluation.

The browser interaction is a deterministic explanation of repository contracts. It is not a provider benchmark. The local commands are the executable evidence.

Continue with [the tool failure and recovery lab](tool-failure-recovery.md), compose the contracts in the [architecture builder](architecture-builder.md), or return to [Build your own harness](../build/README.md).
