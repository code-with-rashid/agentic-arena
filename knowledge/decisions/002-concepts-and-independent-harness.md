# Decision 002: Concepts First and Independent Harness Development

Date: 2026-09-18. Status: accepted.

The user clarified the goal: one entry point across the agentic ecosystem, supporting enough understanding to build a harness from scratch. The maintainer intends to develop a separate harness repository.

Organize navigation by user problems and explanations by concepts. Cover mechanics, minimal examples, alternatives, failure modes, verification, and sourced implementations. Keep portable shared research plus private Obsidian reflections.

Prioritize knowledge structure, navigation, architecture, and an executable loop before specialized experiments. Boundary Response becomes a supporting reliability/security experiment. See [the backlog](../strategy/implementation-backlog.md).

The future harness consumes research and contributes evidence; it receives no preferred tasks, looser scoring, or special recommendations. Educational examples do not become a production runtime accidentally.

This refines [Decision 001](001-ecosystem-hub.md). Revisit when user journeys, maintained evidence, or contributor capacity justify a change.
