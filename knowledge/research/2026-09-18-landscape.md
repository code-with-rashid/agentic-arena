# Research Log: 2026-09-18

## Scope

Reviewed the Agent Sandbox Taxonomy and adjacent evaluation/governance projects, then compared their documented purposes against local Agentic Arena source. Added overview-level references for interoperability, observability, evaluation infrastructure, and complete coding harnesses.

Sources and review depths are recorded in [the catalog](../maps/repositories.md). External benchmark binaries and suites were not executed; no product scores were independently reproduced. Browsed upstream branch pages may be cached or change; revision-pinned inspection is a prerequisite for integration.

## Findings and interpretation

- AST offers useful layer vocabulary and a probe with explicit visibility limits.
- AgentGovBench already covers deterministic governance; new work should have a specific framework-semantics question.
- BoundaryBench connects restrictions with useful task completion.
- AgentDojo addresses model behavior under hostile tool content.
- OASB's metric-withdrawal disclosure illustrates why labels and observations need an independent source.
- Inspect supplies potential reusable infrastructure.
- MCP/A2A, Harbor, OpenHands, and Langfuse expand the lifecycle map; these remain overview-level leads.

Our inference: the strongest immediate addition is framework behavior around a shared denied action, supported by independent effect evidence and paired allowed controls. The broader hub should connect knowledge, experiments, and integrations.

## Corrections to earlier proposal

A shared policy's success cannot be credited to individual frameworks. A callback alone cannot establish complete mediation. Simulated network/file tools do not test OS boundaries. Delegation provenance requires explicit propagation and observation rather than assuming context-local state crosses process boundaries.
